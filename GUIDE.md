# 📖 LinkedIn Outreach Pro: User Guide

Welcome to your automated job application assistant. This guide will help you use the GUI professionally.

## 1. Setting Up (First Time Only)
1. Go to the **Credentials** tab.
2. Fill in your **LinkedIn Email** and **Password**.
3. Fill in your **Gmail Address** and your **16-character App Password**.
4. Select your **Resume PDF** from your computer.
5. Click **Save Settings**.

## 2. Searching for Recruiters
1. Go to the **Home** tab.
2. In the **Keywords** box, type your target roles (e.g., `Java Developer`, `Frontend Developer`).
3. Click **✨ Enhance** to automatically optimize your keywords for the best LinkedIn results.
4. Click **Run Automation**.
5. **Note**: A browser window will open. If LinkedIn asks for a CAPTCHA or verification, solve it manually in that window. The bot will take over once you are on the home feed.

## 3. Reviewing & Sending Emails
There are two ways to send emails:

### Method A: Automatic (Set and Forget)
1. Turn the **Auto-Send Emails Automatically** switch to **ON**.
2. Click **Run Automation**.
3. The bot will find emails and send your application to them immediately.

### Method B: Manual (Safe Mode)
1. Turn the **Auto-Send Emails** switch to **OFF**.
2. Click **Run Automation**.
3. Once the bot finishes, click **Load Saved Emails**.
4. You will see a list of emails in the **Found Emails** box.
5. **Review them**: You can delete any emails you don't like or add new ones manually (separated by commas).
6. Click **Send Mails Now** to message only the people currently in that box.

## 4. Monitoring Progress
- Open the **Logs** tab to see exactly what the bot is doing.
- Green checkmarks (✅) mean an email was sent successfully.
- Red crosses (❌) mean there was an error (usually a bad email address or connection issue).

## 5. Troubleshooting
- **Logs are empty?**: Make sure you clicked "Run Automation" or "Send Mails Now" first.
- **Gmail Fails?**: Double check your **App Password**. Standard passwords do not work.
- **LinkedIn blocks login?**: Open the browser window that the bot launches and log in manually once; the session will be saved for next time.

---
**Tip**: If you want to test the system without bothering real recruiters, put your own email address into the **Found Emails** box and click **Send Mails Now**!
